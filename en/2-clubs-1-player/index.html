2 Clubs → 1 Player

Two clubs, one player to find. Type the name of a footballer who played for both first teams: the first to find one wins the round.

Play nowDownload on the App Store

Free · iPhone and browser · 5 languages

How to play

  1. Two clubs appear, for example Chelsea and Real Madrid.
  2. Type a player who played for both: names are suggested after 3 letters.
  3. Every correct answer counts, and small typos are fine.
  4. First to find one wins the round. Most rounds wins the match.

Game modes

  • Online: face a real player of your level. Every win raises the rating on your player card.
  • Against the AI: practise on your own, at the level you choose.
  • With friends: create a room, share the code and play together.

Levels

Four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. The higher your rating (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Legend), the harder the questions. Every fact is checked against two independent sources.

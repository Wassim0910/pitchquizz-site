How to play

  1. A question appears with four answers.
  2. You have 15 seconds to tap the right one.
  3. Online, your opponent gets the same questions at the same time.
  4. After 7 questions, most correct answers wins; on a tie, the fastest wins.

Game modes

  • Online: face a real player of your level. Every win raises the rating on your player card.
  • Against the AI: practise on your own, at the level you choose.
  • With friends: create a room, share the code and play together.

Levels

Four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. The higher your rating (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Legend), the harder the questions. Every fact is checked against two independent sources.

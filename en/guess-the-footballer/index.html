Guess the player

A footballer's career is revealed club by club, with the years. The sooner you get it the better: the first to name the right player wins the round.

Free · iPhone, Android and browser · 5 languages

How to play

  1. The player's first club appears, with the years.
  2. A new club is added every 5 seconds, until the last one.
  3. Type the name as soon as you recognise the player.
  4. First to find wins the round. On a tie, the fastest overall wins.

Game modes

  • Online: face a real player of your level. Every win raises the rating on your player card.
  • Against the AI: practise on your own, at the level you choose.
  • With friends: create a room, share the code and play together.

Levels

Four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. The higher your rating (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Legend), the harder the questions. Every fact is checked against two independent sources.

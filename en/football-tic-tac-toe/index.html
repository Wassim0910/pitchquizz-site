Football tic-tac-toe

Tic-tac-toe, football style: every square crosses two criteria (a club, a country, a pair of clubs). Name a player who fits both to claim it, and get three in a row.

How to play

  1. The grid shows clubs and countries along the top and side.
  2. Pick a square and name a player who matches both criteria.
  3. Each player can only be used once on the grid.
  4. Three in a row wins. Full grid: most squares at equal turns, then a golden goal.

Game modes

  • Online: face a real player of your level. Every win raises the rating on your player card.
  • Against the AI: practise on your own, at the level you choose.
  • With friends: create a room, share the code and play together.

Levels

Four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. The higher your rating (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Legend), the harder the questions. Every fact is checked against two independent sources.

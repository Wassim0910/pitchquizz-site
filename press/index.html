PITCHQUIZZ

Press kit

In one sentence

PitchQuizz is a free football trivia game where you face real players online, 1v1, in short games: quiz, guess the career, football tic-tac-toe, starting line-ups.

What makes it different

Live duels against real players (not just solo quizzes), a player card rated out of 99 that rises with every win, friend rooms, and data checked against two independent sources. No club logos, no player photos: everything is original artwork.

The games

Quizz · 2 Clubs → 1 Player · Guess the Player · Football Tic-Tac-Toe · Most Players · The XI, plus with friends: Auctions and Undercover.

Facts

Released on the App Store on 3 November 2026 · also playable in the browser at pitchquizz.com · 5 languages (English, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese) · free, no ads · ages 13+ · made in France by Wassim Benhamida.
